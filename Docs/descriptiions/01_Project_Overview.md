01. PROJECT OVERVIEW 

Continuum is an offline-first digital payment system designed to enable users to make and receive digital payments even when internet connectivity is unavailable.

Traditional digital payment systems, such as UPI, generally depend on communication between the user’s device, payment infrastructure and banking servers to authorize transactions.

This creates a problem in areas with poor or no internet connectivity, such as remote villages, mountainous regions, underground locations and network-dead zones.

Continuum aims to address this limitation by introducing a mechanism through which users can perform transactions using previously authorized offline funds, local device communication and cryptographic verification.

Transactions are securely stored on devices and synchronized with the central backend when internet connectivity becomes available.

The ultimate objective is to provide a reliable, secure and convenient digital payment experience without requiring continuous internet connectivity.
