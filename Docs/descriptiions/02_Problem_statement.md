02. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Existing digital payment systems generally require internet connectivity to communicate with banking infrastructure and authorize transactions.

Consequently, users experience difficulties making digital payments in locations where internet connectivity is unreliable or unavailable.

Although offline payment solutions exist, challenges remain in designing systems that can securely manage offline balances, prevent double spending, verify transactions locally and synchronize transaction records when connectivity is restored.

Continuum aims to investigate and implement an offline-first payment infrastructure that addresses these challenges.
